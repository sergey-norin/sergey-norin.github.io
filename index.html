About

I received my Ph.D. from the Georgia Institute of Technology in 2005 in the Algorithms, Combinatorics and Optimization program. My thesis advisor was Robin Thomas.

Before coming to McGill in 2011, I spent a year as a Quantitative Analyst at D. E. Shaw & Co., a year as a Visiting Assistant Professor at Georgia Tech, and four years as an Instructor and Assistant Professor at Princeton University.

My research interests are in graph theory, combinatorics and their applications.
